Welcome to Pioneer Waters! We are the premier provider of exhilarating and enthralling whitewater rafting experiences in the Midwest.

Our reputation is second to none, and our professional guides are all certified and qualified to teach and serve our clients.

The Missouri River is a beautiful but challenging natural phenomenon, and we are committed to revealing its majesty and magnificence to everyone: young and old, big and small.

From complete beginners to expert adventurers, we cater for all requirements.

We have several different trip offers with correspondingly captivating names to whet your water-based appetites, including:

Deep Valley, Devil’s Bend, Freedom Trail, Brigham Gorge.

Each is tailored to a specific experience level, so we have something for all abilities.
