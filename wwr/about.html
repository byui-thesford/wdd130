Pioneer Waters

Situated on the border between Nebraska and Iowa, Pioneer Waters is your perfect companion in
the perfect location. For over 40 years, we have welcomed avid adventurers of all ages to sample
the delights of dynamic whitewater rafting.

History

It was way back in 1984 that the Smith family had a vision: to leave the rat race behind and live near the fresh waters of the Missouri river. Their desire was to retrace the footsteps of their courageous pioneering ancestors who inhabited Winter Quarters 140 years before.

After many hard months of working in and around the local landscape, they finally realised their dream of sharing their love of the water with travellers far and wide. Within view of Omaha's Eppley Airfield and just a mile from Council Bluffs, Pioneer Waters was born in June 1985.
